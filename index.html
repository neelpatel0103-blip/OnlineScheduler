Staff scheduling

Schedule time & holidays

Choose a day and time window to add a shift. Regular bookings add available 30-minute periods under the staffing target; extra/call-in bookings add the full selected window without a team-size limit and are counted separately. Operating hours: weekdays 10:00 am–4:00 am; weekends 11:00 am–4:00 am.

Choose a day and time window to check availability.

Regular bookings fill available time in the selected window, up to current staffing targets. Extra/call-in bookings add the full selected window above the target. Posted time appears on the shared schedule immediately; contact an administrator for corrections.

A “—” means no shift or a selected holiday. Call-in hours are marked EXTRA and included in weekly totals.
